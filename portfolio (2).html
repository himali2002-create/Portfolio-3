What Was the Likely Cause?
The Mistake
  • Overreacted to Year 1 underperformance in a single market
  • Entered Chile & Mexico simultaneously — too fast
  • Reduced advertising intensity per market by spreading budget thin
  • Misjudged the balance between expansion and brand building
Key Insight
  • Sales volume growth ≠ strong brand performance
  • Rapid expansion requires sufficient sustained marketing support
  • Advertising must be consistent and well-funded across each market
  • Balance between geographic expansion and brand equity is essential
What We Changed
Strategy
  • Shifted from aggressive expansion to controlled, phased growth
  • Focused on fewer markets with better resource allocation
  • Prioritised brand building over rapid geographic spread
Marketing
  • Increased and stabilised advertising investment per market
  • Avoided large fluctuations in ad spend between periods
  • Aligned promotion with local market needs
Operations
  • Improved coordination across pricing, promotion & distribution
  • Used performance data to guide every decision
  • Adjusted strategies gradually — no sudden shifts

The Key Lesson
Good budget allocation requires constant adjustments based on data.
  • Budget allocation should be continuously monitored and adjusted based on performance data.
  • Decisions should rely on KPIs — sales, ROI, market share — rather than assumptions.
  • High spending does not always guarantee better results; efficiency matters more than volume.
  • Budgets should be reallocated toward high-performing markets or strategies.
  • Regular analysis identifies underperforming activities early and reduces unnecessary costs.
  • A data-driven approach enables better optimisation and improved profitability over time.
